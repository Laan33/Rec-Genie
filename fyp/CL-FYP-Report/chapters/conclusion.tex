\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:future_work}
The system is designed to be modular, allowing for the addition of new features and the adjustment of existing features.

Theoretically this system could be extended to include a wider range of features, and a wider range of recommendation systems.
It could also be used to provide recommendations for other items, and could also be used to provide recommendations for other types of data.
\\\\
The large language models have a lot of potential, particularly if given the time, right data and resources, the models could be fine-tuned to provide higher quality interactions, and reduce issues such as the noise in the sentiment analysis.

The system could also be extended to include a wider range of features, or utilise a different adjustable recommendation system.
