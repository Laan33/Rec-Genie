\section{User Evaluation Results}
\label{sec:user_evaluation_results}
The user evaluation was conducted with 4 participants, who were asked to complete a series of tasks and provide feedback on the system. The evaluation aimed to assess the system's usefulness, ease of use, ability to understand user preferences, and overall potential. The process is detailed in \cref{sec:user_evaluation}, and the full survey results can be found in \cref{appendix:surveyResults}.

\subsection{Survey Results}
\label{sec:survey_results}
The survey results were overwhelmingly positive, with users highlighting the system's ability to provide relevant recommendations, adapt to changing preferences, and explain its reasoning. Below is a breakdown of the key findings:

\paragraph{System Usefulness:}  
Users rated the system highly in terms of usefulness, with three users giving it a score of 5 and one user giving it a 4. Comments emphasized the system's intuitive nature and its ability to provide reasoned and relevant recommendations. For example, one user stated, \textbf{"The system is a lot more intuitive than Netflix is."} Another user noted the system's ability to remove a disliked film from recommendations after expressing their distaste for its director and genre.

\paragraph{Ease of Use:}  
The system was rated as easy to use, with scores ranging from 1 (not at all cumbersome) to 2. Users appreciated the conversational interface and the simplicity of the prompts. One user remarked, \textbf{"Very easy to use because of the conversational interface with the system."} Another user highlighted the straightforward nature of the prompts, making the system accessible even for first-time users.

\paragraph{Understanding User Preferences:}  
The system demonstrated a strong ability to understand and adapt to user preferences, with three users rating it a 5 and one user rating it a 4. Users praised the system's responsiveness and its ability to incorporate their preferences into recommendations. For instance, one user commented, \textbf{"It really understood my interests and took them into account while recommending."} Another user noted that while some noise was present in the recommendations, the system was highly responsive when relevant filters were applied.

\paragraph{Recommendation Explanations:}  
The system's ability to explain its recommendations was well-received, with scores of 4 and 5 across the board. Users found the explanations clear and concise, though one user suggested adding more detail about user-user similarity, such as a summary of the profiles of similar users. This feedback highlights an opportunity to enhance the transparency of the collaborative filtering process.

\paragraph{Adaptability to Changing Preferences:}  
All users confirmed that the system successfully reflected their changing preferences in its recommendations. For example, one user noted, \textbf{"The system refreshed its recommendations after each preference,"} while another highlighted how the system removed a disliked film from the recommendations after feedback.

\paragraph{Overall Potential:}  
Users rated the system's potential highly, with three users giving it a score of 5 and one user giving it a 4. They appreciated the system's concept, execution, and ability to provide a conversational, adjustable, and explainable recommendation experience. One user described the system as \textbf{"very promising,"} and another stated, \textbf{"I would definitely use this system if it was packaged into a more accessible platform (e.g., an app or website)."}

\subsection{User Feedback}
\label{sec:user_feedback}
The feedback provided by users was instrumental in identifying the system's strengths and areas for improvement.

\paragraph{Positive Feedback:}  
Users praised the system for its intuitive interface, ability to adapt to preferences, and clear explanations. They found the system engaging and appreciated its ability to provide high-quality recommendations. One user described the system as "\textbf{executed very skillfully,"} while another highlighted its \textbf{"vast knowledge and accurate recommendations"}.

\paragraph{Suggestions for Improvement:}  
Users provided constructive feedback for enhancing the system:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Visual Enhancements:} Users suggested including movie posters alongside recommendations to make the interface more visually appealing.
    \item \textbf{User-User Similarity Details:} One user requested a summary or background information about similar users used in collaborative filtering to improve transparency.
    \item \textbf{Suggested Replies:} Another user recommended adding suggested replies under the chatbot to reduce typing effort.
    \item \textbf{Noise Reduction:} While the system was praised for its responsiveness, some users noted occasional noise in the recommendations and suggested refining the filtering process.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Conclusion}
The user evaluation results demonstrate that the system is effective in providing conversational, adjustable, and explainable recommendations. Users found the system engaging, intuitive, and capable of adapting to their preferences. The feedback gathered will be invaluable in guiding future improvements, such as enhancing the user interface, increasing transparency, and refining the recommendation algorithms.
